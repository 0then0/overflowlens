# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in OverflowLens issues, pull requests and other project spaces is expected to treat others respectfully, regardless of experience, identity or background.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, evidence and decisions without attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept good-faith corrections.
- Respect differing viewpoints and people's privacy and boundaries.
- Share only data you have permission to publish; prefer synthetic telemetry in reports.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information without consent, and persistent disruption are unacceptable. These rules also apply when someone represents the project in another public space.

## Reporting and enforcement

For non-sensitive moderation requests, contact the maintainer through a repository issue, describing the behavior and linking to the relevant discussion. Do not repeat private information or abusive material unnecessarily. For harassment, threats or privacy violations that need confidential handling, use [GitHub's abuse reporting form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). GitHub handles those reports under its own policies; the project maintainer does not receive them automatically.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock discussions, warn participants, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation, depending on severity and repetition. Moderation decisions should explain the applicable rule when doing so does not compromise privacy or safety. Participants can request reconsideration by providing relevant new context without disrupting unrelated discussions.
